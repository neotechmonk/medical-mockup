## Menopause and perimenopause (female-oriented only)
Banner: **You're not just getting older.**

| They were told | Reply |
|---|---|
| It's your age. | It's not just your age. |
| Push through it. | You shouldn't have to push through it. |
| It's just hormones. | Hormones are real. So is the help. |
| It's stress. | Stress doesn't explain all of it. |
| Maybe you're depressed. | Maybe it's more than one thing. Let's look at all of it. |
| You're too young for menopause. | Perimenopause can start earlier than you've been told. |

Hooks:
- **Work:** Foggy at your desk? You're not losing it.
- **Relationships:** Snapping at the people you love? Whoever is in your life, the change reaches them too.
- **Mental health:** Anxious, low or flat, and not sure why? Mood changes are part of this for many people and deserve proper attention.
- **The long haul:** This isn't a phase you just wait out. It can run for years.
- **Options:** There's more help than you've been told. Hormonal and non-hormonal options, reviewed with you over time.

Segments:
- **40-45, "Too young for this? Maybe not."** Changes that start in your early 40s are easy to miss and easy to dismiss. We'll take them seriously.
- **45 and up, "Nobody warned you. Let's catch up."** This can last a long time, so let's plan for the long run, not just the next few months.
