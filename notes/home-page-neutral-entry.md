# Neutral home page entry

Direction from the owner (live discussion). It supersedes the earlier choice to use the "Midlife changed the rules" hook on the shared top screen.

## Decisions
- The home page is fully neutral. It does not use "Midlife changed the rules. Let's work out the new ones." as its hero, because that line reads as menopause-specific.
- That line can stay on the menopause landing page (where menopause ads and direct menopause traffic arrive).
- Home page = one punchy opening line plus four thumbnail-style entries. No paragraphs.
- Each thumbnail routes straight into that service's own page. No shared "midlife" framing carries over.
- Inside a service page, the other services are not shown (footer-only).
- Paid or direct service traffic skips the home page and lands on that service's page.

## Opening line options (home page)
The line must not lean toward any one service, and must work as a real entry point, not a softer version of the three categories.

| # | Line | Why |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Whatever it is, you can say it here. | Invites without naming anything; carries the "nothing is too awkward" idea. |
| 2 | Come as you are. We'll take it from there. | Warm, low pressure; suits someone unsure what they need. |
| 3 | Got something you've been meaning to talk to someone about? | Speaks to the postponed conversation all three audiences share. |
| 4 | Time to actually talk. | Short and punchy; states the practice's difference (time to listen). |
| 5 | Not sure where to start? Start here. | Good for the organic browser; also feeds the fourth thumbnail. |

Options 6 (Tell us what's going on) and 7 (You've been brushed off before. Not here.) were dropped by the owner. Preview uses option 1 as a draft, not final.

## Thumbnails (four)
Each is a small image plus one short line.

| Thumbnail | Line | Goes to |
|---|---|---|
| Weight | Not a willpower problem. | Medical weight care page |
| Menopause | You're not just getting older. | Menopause and perimenopause page |
| Sexual health | Intimacy changes. Say it here. | Sexual and intimate health page |
| Something else (open) | None of these? Start here. | Start-the-conversation page |

The fourth is a genuine open door, not a fourth category: for the person who does not fit, or does not want to name it.

Thumbnail imagery direction: small, warm, no faces needed, no scales or tape measures, no bedrooms, domestic textures and soft light.

## Routing rule
- **Organic / home page visitors:** see the opening line and four thumbnails.
- **Google Ads or direct service links:** go straight to the dedicated service page (for example `/menopause`, `/weight`, `/sexual-health`), not the home page.
- The menopause page may keep "Midlife changed the rules. Let's work out the new ones." if it fits there.
- Squarespace: separate pages per service, each with its own URL.
