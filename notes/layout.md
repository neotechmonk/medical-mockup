# Layout

Squarespace single long-scroll landing page, mobile-first.

Every option routes visitors in the first screen: hook headline, "What's brought you here?", four entry paths. Below that: a "time to listen" strip, service sections (menopause split 40-45 and 45+), about Dr Majella, booking CTA, footer note.

Options drawn as greyscale wireframes (desktop and mobile) on the design canvas:
- **A. Three big stacked doors**
- **B. Split hero with a chooser panel**
- **C. Question-led conversation**

Colour is applied only after the palette is chosen.
