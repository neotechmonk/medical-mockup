# Project context

Everything here was stated by the project owner. Update this file when something changes, so nobody has to repeat themselves.

## Brief
- Site: thetimelesspassions.com.au (brand name "MidlifeHealth"). GP clinic site for Dr Majella Soumakiyan, special interest in menopause medicine, psychosexual therapy and weight management.
- Current site copy came from ChatGPT; it needs critical evaluation, including the images (alt text is "Placeholder", filenames suggest AI-generated stock).
- Audiences: menopause/perimenopause, weight management, sexual wellbeing.
- Positioning: an unconventional-practice medical professional who is welcoming and accommodating of people's real issues, with a modern perspective. Gender-neutral where possible, with a female-oriented version for comparison.
- Copy: not wordy, emotionally resonant, focused on wellbeing, cut across marketing and client-segment targeting.
- Imagery: inspiration and direction, not generated images.
- The clinic site is entirely separate from the owner's culinary project. Do not mix them up.

## Audience rules
- Weight management and sexual health: all genders.
- Menopause and perimenopause: people assigned female at birth; female-oriented, no gender-neutral version.
- Wording stays inclusive enough that someone who lands on the wrong content is not lost.
- Landing page has entry calls to action for the three services at the top; each service loads its own content.
- Menopause segments: 40-45 and 45 and up. Consider lifestyle (kids, single) without isolating anyone.

## Client perspective
The reader has probably bounced between GPs, felt unheard, frustrated or judged, and wants to be heard and have things sorted. Lead with being believed, not blamed.

## Hook
"Midlife changed the rules. Let's work out the new ones." Works for all three services.

## Build
- Will be built on Squarespace as a single-page landing layout.
- Squarespace has no native random or per-source content swapping; that would need a small script in Code Injection (Core, Plus or Advanced plan; check Squarespace's own pricing page).
- Public previews come first (this repo, GitHub Pages), then translation to Squarespace.
