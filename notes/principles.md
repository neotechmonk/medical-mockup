# Principles

Drawn from what the project owner has asked for. Add to this, do not rewrite it silently.

1. **Heard, not blamed.** Open with belief and respect. Never imply willpower or age is the whole story.
2. **Unconventional but welcoming.** Unconventional shows up as specific practice (time to listen, real options, no flinching on intimacy), not as attitude.
3. **Honest about audience.** Not everything is for everyone, so route people early, but keep wording inclusive so a wrong landing does not lose them.
4. **Succinct and emotional.** Short lines that speak to the problem and the wellbeing behind it.
5. **Modern options exist.** Menopause is long-running; "push through it" is not the plan. Hormonal and non-hormonal options are discussed over time.
6. **No clinical white, no spa, no pharma.** Warm, grown-up colour. Red is welcome.
7. **Colour and layout are separate concerns.** Decide palette first, then layout.
8. **No invented claims.** No outcomes, statistics, testimonials or credentials beyond what is stated.
9. **Your usual GP remains central.** Say so on the page.
